Drive Duration Explained

Drive duration is calculated using both stroke rate and recovery ratio and represents the amount of time spent on the drive. At 20 spm a full stroke takes 3 seconds. If your ratio is 2:1, then you spend twice as long on the recovery than the drive — the drive is ⅓ the recovery — so the drive duration is 3 sec * 0.33 = 1 sec.

Choosing a Ratio

Classically, new rowers are often instructed to row at a 2:1 or even 3:1 ratio. In a boat, this slow recovery aims to improve boat “run” or reduce “check”. But a slow recovery must mean a fast drive for any given stroke rate: rowing at just 30 spm with a 2:1 ratio yields a drive duration of 0.67 sec.

Many studies have measured maximum drive speeds (shortest drive durations) on indoor rowers and all classes of on-water rowing shells. The fastest boats may achieve about 0.7 sec of drive duration — a very competitive single rowing shell might see 0.9 sec.

Metrownome warns you when you configure a rate & ratio combination resulting in a drive duration of less than 0.8 sec. Of course, you are welcome to use these settings. You may find keeping in sync very difficult — and possibly dangerous to both your body and your workout.

For the Math Nerds

Concept 2 ErgData

The ErgData app displays a Drive Time value. You might see times that are well below the numbers mentioned above. Concept 2’s definition is: “Drive time is measured by the amount of time the flywheel is accelerating.” It’s possible that near the end of the drive, when pulling the hands in, the flywheel has started to decelerate. As a result, the “arms-in” time may not count toward drive time, and would show a lower value.

Slight Discrepancies

You might see drive duration values in Metrownome that are not quite what you expect. The above 2:1 @ 30 spm’s drive duration is shown as 0.64 sec instead of 0.67 sec, for example. The app counts a small amount of time for the duration of the catch and the finish. These amounts are subtracted from the total stroke duration before drive duration is calculated.
